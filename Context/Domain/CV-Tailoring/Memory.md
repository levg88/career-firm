## Facts
<!-- Stable, verified knowledge. One bullet per fact. Tagged. -->

- Pipeline order is fixed: Office Manager → Headhunter → Resume Writer → deterministic grounding validator → PDF + DOCX → SQLite + local files. (General)
- Truthfulness splits facts from presentation: employers, titles, dates, degrees, certifications, locations, metrics and scope are facts; selection, ordering, compression, rephrasing, headline, summary and keyword mirroring are presentation and may be tailored. (General)
- The writer may reorder or compress content but never drops an employer, title, or date. (General)
- The deterministic validator checks that every generated employer, every year in a date range, every number in a bullet, and the email/phone appear in the source CV/context; it also enforces `do_not_claim` exclusions and strips unsupported ATS keywords. (General)
- Grounding is conservative pattern matching, explicitly not semantic proof. (General)
- The job detail page shows a live progress bar (Analyzing → Writing → Rendering) driven by auto-polling of the background run. (General)

## Gotchas
<!-- Traps, false-failure causes, timing quirks. Tagged. -->

- Grounding warnings do not block download: they are shown prominently in the UI and stored alongside the generated CV, so human review is still required. (General)
