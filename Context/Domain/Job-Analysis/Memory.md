## Facts
<!-- Stable, verified knowledge. One bullet per fact. Tagged. -->

- The Headhunter's contract: separate must-haves from nice-to-haves, map each requirement to quoted evidence from the CV/context, assign a match score, recommend the best CV vertical, list gaps, and produce a tailoring plan. (General)
- The Headhunter JSON, the score and the selected CV vertical are persisted with the job and drive the resume-writing step. (General)
- Ingestion and analysis are separable: a job can be stored without an analysis and analysed/tailored later by job id. (General)

## Gotchas
<!-- Traps, false-failure causes, timing quirks. Tagged. -->
