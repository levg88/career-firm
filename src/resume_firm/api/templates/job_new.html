Tailor a CV to a job

Paste the job description or upload it. The headhunter will screen it, pick the strongest CV vertical, and the resume writer will produce a job-specific PDF and DOCX.

What happens next

  • Headhunter: extracts must-haves, ATS keywords, culture signals, red flags, and an evidence-based match score.
  • Resume writer: uses only the selected source CV and your context; preserves employers, titles, dates, and metrics.
  • Grounding check: flags any new employer, year, metric, or contact detail that is not in the source material.
